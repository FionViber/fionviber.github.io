Saltline

A first-person shooter set in a rainy container port.

Open project ↗Source repository ↗

Getting started

Deploy into the port, cut three uplink relays and reach the boat. Use WASD to move, the mouse to aim, E to interact and Esc or P to pause. Arrow keys or IJKL can turn the view if mouse capture is unavailable.

Projects open in their own pages. Where a project stores progress in your browser, export a save before clearing site data or changing devices.
